Overnight Chia

चिया

  • Time 5 min + a night
  • Serves 1
  • Made with Satva Chia Seeds

Three spoons of chia to a cup of milk, sweetened however you take it, left in the fridge overnight. Fruit in the morning. Nothing is cooked and nothing can go wrong.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbspChia Seeds
  • 1 cupmilk (any kind)
  • 1-2 tsphoney or jaggery
  • a pinchcardamom powder
  • ½ cupfresh fruit
  • 1 tbspchopped nuts

Method

  1. Whisk the chia into the milk with the sweetener and cardamom.
  2. Leave 5 minutes, then whisk again - this breaks up the clumps that always form the first time.
  3. Cover and refrigerate overnight, or at least 4 hours.
  4. In the morning, stir, and top with the fruit and nuts.
