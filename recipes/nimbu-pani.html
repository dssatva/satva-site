Nimbu Pani

निंबू पाणी

  • Time 5 min
  • Serves 4
  • Made with Satva Himalayan Pink Salt

Lemon, cold water, a little sugar and just enough pink salt that you notice it. Roasted cumin if you want it to taste like a summer afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 3lemons
  • 4 cupschilled water
  • ½ tspHimalayan Pink Salt
  • 4 tbspsugar or jaggery
  • ½ tspCumin Seeds, roasted and ground
  • a pinchBlack Pepper
  • 8mint leaves
  • to serveice

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar and salt in 1 cup of the water first - they will not dissolve in ice-cold water later.
  2. Roll the lemons on the counter, then squeeze them in.
  3. Add the rest of the water, the cumin and the pepper. Taste: the salt should be just noticeable, not salty.
  4. Bruise the mint leaves, drop them in, and serve over ice.
