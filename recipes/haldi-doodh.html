Haldi Doodh

हळदीचे दूध

  • Time 10 min
  • Serves 2
  • Made with Satva Turmeric

Warm milk, a spoon of turmeric, a pinch of pepper and a little ginger, simmered gently and never boiled hard. What gets made when someone in the house has a cold.

Ingredients

  • 2 cupsfull-fat milk
  • 1 tspTurmeric
  • a pinchBlack Pepper, freshly crushed
  • ½ inchginger, grated
  • 1 small stickcinnamon
  • 2 tspjaggery or honey
  • ½ tspghee

Method

  1. Put the milk, turmeric, pepper, ginger and cinnamon in a small pan and whisk so the turmeric does not clump.
  2. Bring it up to a gentle simmer and keep it there for 5 minutes, whisking now and then. Do not let it boil hard.
  3. Strain into two cups and stir in the ghee.
  4. Sweeten once it is warm rather than hot - honey especially should never go into boiling milk.
