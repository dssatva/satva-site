Chicken Curry

चिकन करी

  • Time 50 min
  • Serves 4
  • Made with Satva Coriander Powder

A home chicken curry, built on onions browned slowly and plenty of coriander powder, which thickens the gravy as much as it flavours it.

Ingredients

  • 750 gchicken, curry cut
  • 3 tbspoil
  • 1 tspCumin Seeds
  • 1bay leaf
  • 2onions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbspginger-garlic paste
  • 2tomatoes, puréed
  • 2½ tspCoriander Powder
  • 1 tspChilli Powder
  • ½ tspTurmeric
  • 1 tspGaram Masala
  • ¼ cupcurd, whisked
  • to tastesalt
  • 2 tbspcoriander leaves, chopped

Method

  1. Heat the oil, add the cumin seeds and bay leaf, then the onions. Brown them slowly, 12-15 minutes. This is where the colour of the gravy comes from.
  2. Add the ginger-garlic paste and fry for 2 minutes, then the tomato purée. Cook until the oil separates, about 8 minutes.
  3. Lower the heat, add the coriander powder, chilli powder and turmeric, and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the chicken and salt and fry for 5 minutes on high, so the masala sticks to it.
  5. Take the pan off the heat for a moment and stir in the curd so it does not split. Add 1½ cups of hot water, cover and simmer for 20 minutes until the chicken is done.
  6. Stir in the garam masala, rest for 5 minutes, and finish with coriander leaves.
