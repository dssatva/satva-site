    Giving back

    Satva Saheli Empowering women. Enriching kitchens. सत्व सहेली

    A saheli is the friend a woman actually turns to. Purity begins at home — so instead of a factory network, we work with women in their own communities, and the work and the wage stay with them.

    Selected ingredients are roasted and blended at home by women using their own family recipes. Every Saheli is trained in FSSAI food safety, hygiene and nutrition before she packs anything.

    Ways to be a Saheli

    • The Satva Maker Roasts and blends the traditional masalas at home, to recipes that were already in the family.
    • The Satva Promoter Carries healthy staples to the households around her — a neighbourhood at a time, not a warehouse.
